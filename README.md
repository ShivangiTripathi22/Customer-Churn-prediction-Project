# Customer Segmentation & Churn Pattern Analytics in European Banking

## Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges in the banking industry, as losing existing customers directly impacts revenue, customer lifetime value, and business growth. This project analyzes customer churn behavior in a European banking dataset through a segmentation-driven analytical approach.

The objective of the project is to identify customer groups with higher churn risk and understand how churn varies across geography, demographics, financial profiles, customer engagement levels, and banking product usage. Using exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation techniques, KPI analysis, and interactive dashboarding, the project uncovers actionable insights that can support customer retention strategies and business decision-making.

## Key Objectives

* Measure the overall customer churn rate.
* Analyze churn patterns across different customer segments.
* Compare churn behavior across European regions (France, Germany, and Spain).
* Identify high-risk customer groups based on age, tenure, balance, and credit score.
* Evaluate the impact of customer engagement and product usage on churn.
* Assess revenue risk associated with high-value customer attrition.

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured analytics workflow:

1. Data Cleaning and Validation

   * Dataset quality assessment
   * Missing value analysis
   * Data consistency checks

2. Customer Segmentation

   * Geographic Segmentation
   * Age-Based Segmentation
   * Credit Score Categorization
   * Balance-Based Segmentation
   * Tenure-Based Segmentation

3. Churn Analysis

   * Overall churn rate calculation
   * Segment-wise churn comparison
   * Customer profile comparison between churned and retained customers

4. Financial Risk Assessment

   * High-value customer churn analysis
   * Revenue-at-risk estimation
   * Salary and balance relationship analysis

5. Business Intelligence Dashboard

   * Interactive KPI monitoring
   * Dynamic filtering and drill-down analysis
   * Churn trend visualization across customer segments

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Overall Churn Rate
* Segment-wise Churn Rate
* Geographic Risk Index
* High-Value Customer Churn Ratio
* Revenue at Risk
* Engagement Drop Indicator
